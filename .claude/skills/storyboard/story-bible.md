# Output templates and a worked example

Use these formats at each step of the storyboard skill.

---

## Concept card (step 2)

> **3. Surviving 365 Days Underground With My Toddler** · 31/35 (recommended)
> Curiosity 5 · Stakes 5 · Specificity 5 · Identification 4 · Novelty 4 · Escalation 5 · Visual 3
> - **Open question:** Can a 2-year-old stay silent when the dead walk overhead? Who is knocking?
> - **Thumbnail idea:** a sealed steel bunker door lit by one red alert light, a small hand-drawn
>   calendar taped beside it.
> - **Risk:** the bunker is visually repetitive. Vary it with close-ups of gauges and objects, and
>   with the surface trip.

---

## Packaging (step 3)

> **Pair A (recommended)**
> - **Title:** Surviving 365 Days Underground With My Toddler (47 chars)
> - **Thumbnail:** a heavy steel bunker door in darkness, one red alert light above it, a toddler's
>   stuffed rabbit on the concrete floor in front of the door. Cold blue shadows, the red light the
>   only warm colour.
> - **Thumbnail text:** DAY 365 (top left)
> - **Curiosity gap:** Why is the rabbit outside the door, and what's behind it?
> - **Image prompt:** *Photorealistic cinematic still, a heavy steel blast door in a dark concrete
>   bunker, a single red emergency light glowing above it, a small worn stuffed rabbit sitting on the
>   floor in front of the door, cold blue shadows, strong contrast, the door filling the right two
>   thirds of the frame, empty dark wall top left, 35mm, film grain*
> - **Promise:** a parent and a small child survive a year in a bunker during an outbreak, and
>   something reaches the door.
> - **Description, first 2 lines:** When the emergency broadcast cut out, we had 4 minutes to get
>   Theo into the bunker. This is how we lasted 365 days, and who was knocking on Day 365.

## Story bible (step 4)

```
PROTAGONIST   Name, age, job · WANT · NEED · FLAW · VOICE (how they talk, 1 sample line)
DEPENDENT     Who, age, one endearing habit, one vulnerability
OTHERS        Name · role · secret or arc (2–3 characters at most)
ANCHOR OBJECTS
  1. Object · first appearance (setup) · return (danger) · payoff
WORLD RULES & NUMBERS
  Threat rules (how it spreads, what it reacts to) · resources with amounts and burn rate ·
  shelter specs (size, air, power)
THREAT LADDER
  1. Problem · dimension (external / inside / body / resource / group / mind / moral) · solution · cost
CENTRAL OPEN LOOP   The question · where it's planted · the answer · where it's answered
ENDING              Climax · twist (which setup it pays off) · last line
SETUPS → PAYOFFS    Setup (beat) → payoff (beat)
CAST & LOCATIONS    In timeline-generation's format: name + full visual description
```

## Worked example: *365 Days Underground* (12 min)

**Premise:** A father, his wife Jen and their 2-year-old son Theo spend a year in a backyard bunker
during an outbreak.
**Central loop:** "On Day 365, something knocked. And it knew Theo's name." Planted at 0:24, answered
at 11:40.

| Day | Escalation (dimension) | Tactical solution | Cost → next problem | Specifics |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The outbreak; 4 minutes to get in (external) | Bunker sealed, resource math | Theo's rabbit left upstairs (open loop) | 500 gal water ≈ 5.5 months; 2,100 kcal/day |
| 12 | Footsteps directly above (external + inside) | The "Quiet Game": a 2-year-old must stay silent | Jen hasn't slept in 3 days | The noise rule: no sound over a whisper after dark |
| 88 | Theo, 103 °F fever (body) | Rationed antibiotics, labelled in Jen's handwriting | Half the medicine gone | The fever breaks after 3 days, then violence: is it the infection? |
| 150 | Midpoint: the radio says the infection is airborne (the truth changes) | Seal the vents | The air starts going bad | CO₂ monitor on the wall |
| 214 | CO₂ at 1,800 ppm and climbing (resource) | A forced trip to the surface to clear the air intake | The father is scratched? (open loop) | Cleared in 6 minutes; 40 metres to the intake |
| 300 | Lowest point: Jen wants to leave (group + moral) | They vote and stay | Trust cracked | 61 gallons left |
| 365 | The knock, the voice says "Theo" (mind) | Twist: Theo's rabbit is pushed through the hatch | Who has been watching them? Sequel hook | |

**Interrupts used:** a Title card at every day change, radio static that cuts out, the CO₂ alarm,
whispered lines, a light Shake on footsteps, close-ups of the calendar, the gauge and the rabbit, and a
short flipbook of dust falling from the ceiling (3 frames of the same shot).
