# Storyboard: I Was the Last Person on Earth for 1,000 Days. Then Someone Knocked

> Fiction. A psychological thriller told by Cal Mercer, 44, a lineman who has been alone on Earth
> with his old dog for 1,000 days since everyone (and his wife June) vanished at 3:07 a.m. Then the
> knock comes: June's private knock, at 3:07, one door closer each night. YouTube 16:9, about 10.5
> minutes. Built with the storyboard skill; load it with timeline-generation.
